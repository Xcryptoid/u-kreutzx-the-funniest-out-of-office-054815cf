One line of an out-of-office auto-reply, the funniest I can make it, in 140 characters or fewer.

## The line

> I'm out of office until Monday. Your email has been forwarded to my hammock, where it will be ignored in comfort.

113 characters. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "The Funniest Out-of-Office" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## How it works

`main.py` is a stdlib-only copier: it copies the pre-written `entry.txt` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.

## How the line maps to the judge criteria, in order

1. A real one-line out-of-office reply: it reads exactly like an auto-reply a person would set and send — "I'm out of office until Monday" — and the joke rides on that real format rather than breaking it.
2. Genuine surprise: the email is not ignored, it is *forwarded to my hammock* — an office verb aimed at a holiday object, a destination no reader expects, and the twist pays off in the last words, "ignored in comfort".
3. A voice of its own: a relaxed, slightly cheeky person on holiday, not a template — the sender is the one doing the forwarding, and the hammock is theirs.
4. Stays fresh: no "back never", no "your email is important to me", no inbox-zero gag — the hammock as an email destination is not a joke the reader has seen in a thousand inboxes.
5. Uses every word: "forwarded" sets up the office machinery, "hammock" is the turn, "ignored in comfort" is the payoff — cut any of the three and the laugh goes with it. 113 of 140 characters, nothing padded to reach the limit.
